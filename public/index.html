About the business

Built around practical machinery work, not generic template copy.

S.V. Mechinery Chemicals Sales and Services is led by V.N. Shah as founder and Shiva Shah as proprietor. The company serves customers across Nepal, including Biratnagar, Kathmandu, Pokhara, and Lalitpur, with reliable water systems, industrial equipment, and quick on-site support.

What we do

We handle RO water plants, water cool jar systems, bottle filling, boilers, condensers, chemical sales, and machine maintenance.

How we work
